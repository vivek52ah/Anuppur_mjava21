🚀 HOW TO USE:

   1. Restart your application from Eclipse
   2. Open: http://localhost:8085/anuppur/swagger-ui.html
   3. Click "Authorize" button (lock icon)
   4. Enter username and password
   5. Select an API group from dropdown
   6. Click on any endpoint to expand
   7. Click "Try it out" to test
   8. Fill parameters and click "Execute"
   9. View the response!
